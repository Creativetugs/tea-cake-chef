Baked like Big Mama made them

A Houston trailer with a family recipe and a loyal following.

The Tea Cake Chef makes old-fashioned Southern tea cakes: fluffy, buttery, golden at the edge, and soft in the middle. People drive across town, and come back with another dozen before the first one is gone.

The yellow trailer has been part of Houston’s food-truck scene for more than ten years. Family and friends, church tables, weeknight cravings, and “just one more” from the parking lot.
